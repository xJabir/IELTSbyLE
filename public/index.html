Your IELTS preparation starts here

IELTSbyLE brings study resources, practice and mock tests together in one convenient platform.

Learn

Access study materials prepared by your teachers for Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Learn important strategies, understand question types and strengthen the skills you need for IELTS.

Practise

Put your knowledge into practice with carefully designed IELTS exercises and mock tests. Become familiar with the test format and improve your confidence through regular practice.

Prepare for all four IELTS modules

Develop every skill you need for the IELTS test with dedicated learning and practice for each module.

Listening

Understand what you hear

Practise different listening situations and question types while developing your ability to identify key information and follow conversations effectively.

Reading

Read with confidence

Develop your reading skills through passages and exercises that help you improve comprehension, vocabulary, time management and question solving techniques.

Writing

Write with purpose

Learn how to approach IELTS Writing Task 1 and Task 2. Practise organising ideas, developing arguments and writing clear and effective answers.

Speaking

Speak with confidence

Prepare for all three parts of the IELTS Speaking test. Improve fluency, vocabulary, pronunciation and your ability to express ideas clearly.

More than just a mock test

IELTSbyLE is designed to support your preparation from learning new concepts to practising them and checking your progress.

Learn from your teacher

Your teacher can upload study materials, create practice content and provide guidance based on what you need to improve.

Experience realistic practice

Take computer based mock tests in an environment designed to help you become comfortable with the IELTS test experience before the real examination.

Track your progress

Keep your test results together in your profile so you can understand your performance and monitor your improvement over time.
