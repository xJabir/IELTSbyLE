Prepare for all four IELTS modules

Build your skills across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking with focused resources and practice.

Listening

Understand and listen

Develop your listening skills with structured practice, audio based activities, question types, and mock test sections designed to help you become more confident with spoken English.

Reading

Read with confidence

Practise different IELTS reading question types and learn how to find information, understand passages, manage time, and approach challenging questions more effectively.

Writing

Write for your target band

Practise Task 1 and Task 2, improve your structure and vocabulary, and learn how to develop clear and effective answers with guidance from your teacher.

Speaking

Speak with confidence

Prepare for all three parts of the IELTS Speaking test and practise with teacher guided speaking sessions and scheduled speaking opportunities.
