Two ways to prepare, in one account

Build the skill, then prove it under real conditions — both live in your student dashboard.

Resources

A growing library of PDF study material, organised by module — Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — written and uploaded by your own teacher, not a generic question bank.

Mock Tests

Full-length, computer-delivered mock exams on the same test screen as the real thing, auto-marked where possible and graded by your teacher for Writing — with a genuine band score every time.

Four modules, marked the way IELTS marks them

Every mock test is built from real exam components — reading passages, audio sections, and writing tasks — scored on the same 9-band scale examiners use. Speaking is scheduled rather than recorded.

60 min

Reading

Three passages of increasing difficulty, with the full range of question types: True/False/Not Given, multiple choice, matching, and gap-fill. Auto-marked the moment you submit.

30 min

Listening

Recordings play once, exactly as they do on test day, while you work through four sections of questions in the same navigator you'll see in reading.

60 min

Writing

Task 1 (report or letter) and Task 2 (essay), with a live word counter. Marked by your teacher against the official band descriptors, with written feedback.

Scheduled

Speaking

No recordings to submit — instead, book a live speaking slot with your teacher. Pick an open date and time; once a day's slots are full, it's marked unavailable.

Built for the exam hall, not a quiz app

Most mock test sites are multiple-choice quizzes with a shiny wrapper. This one is built the way the actual exam software works.

A faithful test screen

Grey chrome, a running countdown, and a numbered question navigator at the bottom — so the first time you see this interface isn't on the actual test day.

Your teacher builds the material

Study PDFs, passages, audio, and questions are written and uploaded by your own instructor — not a generic question bank — so both practice and prep track what you're actually being taught.

One profile, every result

Every mock test you sit lands in your profile with a per-module and overall band, so you and your teacher can see progress test over test, not just one score in isolation.
